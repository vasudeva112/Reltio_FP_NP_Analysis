Limitations

  • The independent re-evaluation imitates Reltio's matching from its documentation and the tenant configuration. It has not yet been checked against the tenant itself.
  • The search for missed matches only compares records that share at least one similar value; duplicates that share none would not be found.
  • Reltio's name, noise-word and alias dictionaries were not available, so values are compared without nickname or alias mapping.
  • Working from the Potential Match Report alone, an empty value cannot be told apart from a missing one, and repeated attributes (e.g. several identifiers) lose their grouping.
  • Only steward-confirmed pairs are certain; every other category is an assessment.
